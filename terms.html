Viamour ("we", "us") is the brand and operator behind a portfolio of AI-generated short-form video channels, including Marmalade Pixels. Viamour generates and publishes short-form video content (the "Content") to social media platforms, including TikTok, on behalf of the channels it operates — currently @marmaladepixels.

Use of the platform integration

Viamour's TikTok app is used to publish a channel's own original Content to that channel's own connected TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API, and to read that same account's own video list and engagement stats via TikTok's Login Kit / Display API, after the account owner explicitly authorizes the connection. It does not act on behalf of, or access data for, any TikTok account that has not authorized Viamour.
