Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Viamour ("we", "us") is the brand and operator behind a portfolio of AI-generated short-form video channels, published to social media platforms including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. This policy covers Viamour's app registered with TikTok and any other platform integration Viamour operates on behalf of the channels it runs — including channels Viamour produces for outside creators and businesses (see our Contact page), not only channels Viamour owns itself.

What we collect

Viamour's TikTok integration requests only the OAuth scopes needed to operate the channel accounts connected to it:

  • video.publish — to publish a channel's own video content to its own connected TikTok account.
  • user.info.basic — to display the connected account's nickname and avatar back to the person who connected it.
  • user.info.stats and video.list — to read that same connected account's own follower/like counts and its own published videos' view/like/comment/share counts, shown on our Analytics page.

Each account is connected only after its owner explicitly authorizes Viamour via TikTok's own OAuth consent screen. We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok viewers or other TikTok users — only the credentials and aggregate stats belonging to the channel accounts Viamour itself operates.

How tokens are used

Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are used exclusively to publish a channel's own Content to that channel's own connected TikTok account and to read that same account's own stats back to it, and are stored securely for those purposes only. They are never shared with third parties.

Third-party services

Video content is generated using a third-party AI generation service before being published to TikTok; no TikTok user data is shared with that service.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
