Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Viamour ("we", "us") is the brand and operator behind a portfolio of AI-generated short-form video channels, including Marmalade Pixels, published to social media platforms including TikTok under the account @marmaladepixels. This policy covers Viamour's app registered with TikTok and any other platform integration Viamour operates on behalf of its channels.

What we collect

Viamour's TikTok integration requests only the minimum OAuth scope needed to publish video content to the TikTok accounts it operates (video.publish), currently @marmaladepixels. Each account is connected only after its owner explicitly authorizes Viamour via TikTok's own OAuth consent screen. We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok viewers or other TikTok users — only the credentials (access and refresh tokens) needed to publish to the channel accounts Viamour itself operates.

How tokens are used

Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are used exclusively to publish a channel's own Content to that channel's own connected TikTok account, and are stored securely for that sole purpose. They are never shared with third parties.

Third-party services

Video content is generated using a third-party AI generation service before being published to TikTok; no TikTok user data is shared with that service.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
