Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Viamour ("we", "us") is the brand and operator behind a portfolio of AI-generated short-form video channels, published to social media platforms including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. This policy covers Viamour's apps registered with TikTok and Google (YouTube), and any other platform integration Viamour operates on behalf of the channels it runs — including channels Viamour produces for outside creators and businesses (see our Contact page), not only channels Viamour owns itself.

What we collect

Viamour operates two social-platform integrations. Each requests only the OAuth scopes needed to operate the channel accounts connected to it.

TikTok

  • video.publish — to publish a channel's own video content to its own connected TikTok account.
  • user.info.basic — to display the connected account's nickname and avatar back to the person who connected it.
  • user.info.stats and video.list — to read that same connected account's own follower/like counts and its own published videos' view/like/comment/share counts, shown on our Analytics page.

Each TikTok account is connected only after its owner explicitly authorizes Viamour via TikTok's own OAuth consent screen. We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok viewers or other TikTok users — only the credentials and aggregate stats belonging to the channel accounts Viamour itself operates.

YouTube (Google)

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to publish a channel's own video content to its own connected YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly — to read that same connected channel's own performance data (views, watch time, subscriber changes, likes, comments, shares) for videos it has published, used internally to guide future content decisions.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to list a connected channel's own existing videos, shown on our YouTube page, and to reconcile past uploads with our internal records.

Each YouTube channel is connected only after its owner explicitly authorizes Viamour via Google's own OAuth consent screen. We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to YouTube viewers or other YouTube users — only the credentials and channel-level data belonging to the channel accounts Viamour itself operates.

How we use your data

We use the data described above exclusively to operate the channel account you've connected: publishing content you've approved, confirming it published successfully, and showing that channel's own real videos and performance data back to its operator on our Analytics and YouTube pages, to inform future content decisions. We do not use your TikTok or Google user data for advertising, to build advertising profiles, to train any AI model, or for any purpose unrelated to operating your own connected channel.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your TikTok or Google user data with any third party, advertiser, or data broker. Video content is generated using a third-party AI video generation service before being published — the prompts and generated video files pass through that service, but your TikTok or Google account credentials, access tokens, and account data are never sent to it, or to any other third-party service. We may disclose data if required to do so by law.

How we protect your data

Access and refresh tokens are stored only in access-controlled, private infrastructure: Cloudflare's KV storage (encrypted at rest) for this website's own session data, and a private, access-restricted repository for our automation pipeline's credentials. Tokens are never exposed to any public-facing page, client-side code, or third party. All communication with TikTok's and Google's APIs happens over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Session identifiers stored in your browser are marked HttpOnly and Secure, meaning they cannot be read by page scripts and are only ever sent over an encrypted connection. Access to stored credentials is limited to the operators of the specific channel account they belong to.

Data retention and deletion

We retain a connected channel's access and refresh tokens, and the associated channel data described above, for as long as that channel remains connected to Viamour. You may disconnect a channel, and request deletion of all associated data, at any time — by clicking "Disconnect" on our Analytics or YouTube page, by revoking Viamour's access directly from your TikTok or Google account's own security settings, or by contacting support@viamour.com. Any of these immediately invalidates our stored token. We permanently delete a disconnected channel's stored credentials and associated data within 30 days of disconnection or a deletion request.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
