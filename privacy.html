Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Marmalade Pixels ("we", "us") operates an automated content pipeline that publishes short-form video content to social media platforms, including TikTok, under the account @marmaladepixels.

What we collect

Our TikTok integration requests only the minimum OAuth scope needed to publish video content to our own connected TikTok account (video.publish). We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok users other than the credentials (access and refresh tokens) needed to operate our own account's publishing.

How tokens are used

Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are used exclusively to publish Marmalade Pixels' own Content to its own account, and are stored securely for that sole purpose. They are never shared with third parties.

Third-party services

Video content is generated using a third-party AI generation service before being published to TikTok; no TikTok user data is shared with that service.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
